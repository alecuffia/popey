Why sail here

Barcelona is the Mediterranean at its most alive. Set out for a morning across the Balearic Sea, drop anchor for lunch off Sitges, and return for sunset at the marina.

  • Warm season from May through October
  • Sailboats, motor yachts, and catamarans available
  • Licensed captains and instant booking supported
  • Direct chat with the boat owner before your trip

Top marinas

Departures from the most iconic starting points around the city.

  • Port Olímpic — city marina, easy access from Barceloneta
  • Port Vell — historic harbor at the base of Las Ramblas
  • Port Ginesta — full-service marina, ~25 min from center
  • Marina Vela — luxury docks with concierge service
